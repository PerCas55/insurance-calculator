# insurance-calculator

## Specifications

HTML5 calculator for a car insurance. User enter:

* Age
* kw of the car
* Number of years of driving experience
* Price of the new car
* Coverage (only third party, third party and fire and theft, or fully comprehensive)

Calcuator will immediately return the price of the insurance based on the data entered by the user. 

## Tech stack

The project is built using HTML5, CSS, and JavaScript. Single-page HTML5 application with no backend.
